# Existing skills and creative tools

Survey: 2026-11-22. [JUDGEMENT] Decisions below are recommendations, not hands-on quality or security audits.

## Decisions summary

- **Adapt, do not import a marketing persona wholesale:** use Marketing Skills' social, copy and launch workflows as ingredients for evidence-grounded, platform-specific drafts. Generate several honest hooks, not claims of guaranteed virality. Humanizer is already installed and is the preferred prose editing pass.
- **Reuse specialized creation tools as tools:** the owner's `arch-diagram` and draw.io skill, Mermaid/Excalidraw CLIs, and official Remotion skills are useful without inventing another renderer. Anthropic's creative skills are strong starting points for posters and styling; OpenAI's image skill has a real provider-specific runtime dependency. Editable source and accessible exports are product requirements, not automatic properties of an image model.
- **Build the product's differentiators:** source-to-claim evidence ledger, destination policy gate and current-spec updater, platform-native content rubrics, branded multi-slide composition, export to Market Blaster, and human approval. Existing skills do not establish this end-to-end combination. [JUDGEMENT]
- **Keep destinations and providers separate:** Typefully is an optional integration, not a required backend. Follow Market Blaster's approved [destination policy](https://github.com/edwinjosechittilappilly/market-blaster/blob/main/docs/design/auth-and-reach.md): HN gets original title and URL only, no generated text; restricted communities get the permitted manual/outline workflow. Refresh against [HN's guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) and other first-party rules before delivery.
- **Do not equate scores with reach or AI detection.** Use scores as actionable human-reviewed editorial feedback, with evidence and uncertainty; no tool surveyed proves a post will go viral or reliably identifies AI authorship. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing) says its signs are descriptive and cautions against relying on detectors.

## Gaps: skills to create

1. **Evidence-to-story:** ingest idea, links, PR/commit or Market Blaster brief; build claim-to-source records, distinguish shipped/planned and unsupported facts, preserve quotes, dates and limitations. No fabricated benchmark, first-person experience or endorsement.
2. **Destination policy and format packs:** versioned first-party source URLs, check dates, AI-policy gate, supported formats, accessibility and size constraints; perform no generated HN text and enforce stricter community-specific restrictions. Build local command plus scheduled CI to report source changes and propose reviewable updates, never silently assert old limits are current.
3. **Audience, hooks and platform writers:** developer/founder voice profile, audience intent and honest multi-hook alternatives; LinkedIn/X threads and platform-specific social/article variants. Mark heuristics and do not promise virality.
4. **Brand kit, logo, poster and carousel:** user-owned tokens, font and image rights, per-slide editable source, readable text, alternative text, coherent ordering, platform-safe export and human visual inspection. Native vector logo workflow and trademark checks need human review.
5. **Video brief and producer:** evidence-linked script and storyboard; choose appropriate renderer, produce editable scene source, accessible captions/transcript, inspect representative frames and verify encoded output against current platform requirements.
6. **Independent platform and media reviewer:** score hook, audience fit, simplicity, anti-slop, platform fit, accessibility and grounding separately. Use 1–5 anchored scales with observed evidence, concrete revision and uncertainty; deterministic policy/format/unsupported-claim failures block regardless of overall score. Do not infer audience capture from prose alone. Audit against held-out human-labeled examples and measure agreement; randomize/swap pairwise order when comparing candidates.
7. **Market Blaster handoff:** portable manifest with separate approved variants, sources, asset references, alt text, policy disposition and versions; validate against the actual receiving CLI contract, preserve human approval, distinguish export from publication. No extra scheduler is required. [Existing integration research](https://github.com/edwinjosechittilappilly/market-blaster/blob/main/docs/research/existing-skills-and-tools.md).
