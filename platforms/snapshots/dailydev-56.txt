This document is your comprehensive guide to understanding the core principles and standards that shape content on our platform. Whether you’re a content creator, developer, or enthusiast, these guidelines reflect our commitment to quality, innovation, and ethics. To learn more about how to feature your content on daily.dev, see the guide on getting featured . To see what decides whether a post reaches the wider feed, read What developers on daily.dev respond to . In this document, you’ll find detailed guidance on what content we look for, what we prioritize, and the conditions under which we might remove content. These guidelines provide a reference for anyone who creates or submits content to daily.dev and insight into our dedication to a respectful, diverse, and valuable community. Take a moment, dive in, and let's build an inspiring space together! Tip Representing a company? Company blogs can't be added as sources, but you can bring your content to daily.dev with a Squad. A Verified Company Squad (paid) can auto-post from your RSS feeds and YouTube channels. A regular Squad (free) is one you post to yourself. See company and personal blogs to compare them. Tip Aligning your content with our standards enhances our community’s quality and builds your reputation as a trusted source of knowledge. Non-compliance may lead to reduced reputation points. For more on reputation, see our reputation guide . What content are we looking for? ✅ Relevant, fresh, and up-to-date content for developers. ✅ Professional and well-produced textual or video content. ✅ Innovative, trending content that inspires developers. ✅ Content adhering to ethical publishing practices. ✅ Content from sources that publish regularly. ✅ Content from established sources with substantial published material. ✅ Content that is open about the author's affiliations. ✅ Professional and constructive content Info To maintain a balanced content inventory, we’re temporarily prioritizing niche sources. This helps us provide diverse and unique topics for our developer community. Mainstream content may be given lower priority to keep the mix balanced. When will we reject, ban, or remove content? ❌ Content irrelevant to developers. ❌ Content used primarily for self-promotion. ❌ Content made primarily for SEO or AI visibility (AEO, GEO): posts written to rank, to get cited by AI tools, or to earn backlinks rather than to inform developers. ❌ Content generated through programmatic SEO or methods that populate content automatically. ❌ Paid or sponsored content distributed outside our official programs. See sponsored and paid content . ❌ Non-English content. ❌ Content from inactive sources (three months or more without updates). ❌ Clickbait or misleading content that lacks substantial value. (Readers on Plus can also turn on Clickbait Shield to rewrite misleading titles.) ❌ Content from sources without a publicly accessible RSS or Atom feed. ❌ Politically-oriented content. ❌ AI-generated content, automated algorithms, or content with characteristics typical of AI-generated material. ❌ Content that is illegal, explicit, offensive, or incites discrimination or violence. ❌ Content violating privacy rights or using shortened or redirected URLs. ❌ Content unsuitable for daily.dev's business reputation. ❌ Content infringing third-party rights, including copyright, trademarks, or other IP rights. ✋ For company or personal blogs: Additional terms apply. See below for details. Sponsored and paid content Paid distribution on daily.dev is only permitted through our official programs: Sponsored Content , ads , and Boost . Content in these programs is labeled as sponsored, promoted, or similar, and follows these guidelines like everything else on the platform. Anything else that someone pays for is not allowed: ❌ Posting, commenting on, or upvoting content you were paid or otherwise compensated to promote, whether with money, free products, commission, or any other benefit. This includes posts placed by agencies, freelancers, and influencers on behalf of a client. ❌ Selling or accepting payment for placement. Squad admins and moderators can't charge to publish, approve, or pin posts, and no one may broker or resell placement in daily.dev feeds, Squads, or comments. ❌ Buying or selling engagement, such as upvotes, comments, Awards, or followers. ❌ Affiliate or referral links, and any other links that earn you a commission. ❌ Undisclosed ads presented as tutorials, news, or independent reviews. ❌ Sponsored posts from indexed sources. Articles a publication was paid to run are not eligible, whether or not they are labeled as sponsored, and we may remove them when we become aware of them. Violations can lead to content removal, restricted submissions, and suspension of the account or Squad involved. Company and personal blogs Company blogs Company blogs are not eligible as sources on daily.dev. Learn more about why we moved company sources to Squads . To get your company's content in front of developers, choose between two kinds of Squad: Verified Company Squad Regular Squad Publishing Can auto-post from your RSS feeds and YouTube channels You post to it yourself Price Paid. Talk to our team Free. Create a Squad See Verified Company Squads for what a verified Squad includes. Both kinds of Squad follow these guidelines, including the rules on sponsored and paid content . Verification changes how your Squad is presented, not what you can post in it. Personal blogs Personal blogs are not eligible as sources either. Personal bloggers and indie content creators can create a Squad or share their posts directly with the "New Post" button. If a publication we already index runs your posts, claim ownership of them to get author credit and analytics. Update regarding AI-generated content At daily.dev, we emphasize human insight, expertise, and lived experiences in content creation. We value the unique perspectives and nuanced understanding human authors bring, which strengthens the authenticity and quality of information on our platform. AI-generated content, while advancing in quality, doesn’t meet our standard for human insight, and we prioritize human-created content to maintain genuine engagement. We appreciate your understanding and adherence to this policy. To see how this policy and the rest of these guidelines are enforced in practice, read about content moderation .
