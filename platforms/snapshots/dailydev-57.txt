There are four ways to get your content in front of developers on daily.dev. Whichever you use, your content has to follow the content guidelines . Way Who it's for Cost Post it yourself Anyone with a daily.dev account Free Run a Squad Creators, communities and companies that want a home for their content Free, or paid for a Verified Company Squad Suggest a source Owners of established developer publications. Not company or personal blogs Free Get credit for your articles Authors whose articles daily.dev already picked up Free Post it yourself Click New post , or the pencil button in the sidebar, to open the composer: Pick the type at the bottom left (1): Free form to write a post on daily.dev, Share a link to share an article, or Poll . Choose where it goes at the top (2): Everyone (your profile) and/or up to three Squads you can post in. Click Post , or schedule it (3) for later with the calendar button. Your post is attributed to you, and upvotes on it build your reputation . Note Posts to Everyone from accounts with less than 250 reputation appear on your profile, but don't reach other people's feeds until you reach 250. Posting in an active Squad is a good way to start. Direct posting replaced Community Picks, which was retired in 2025. Run a Squad A Squad is a group on daily.dev where you and other developers post and discuss content around a topic. It's the best fit when you publish regularly and want a following: Create your Squad , then grow it . Make it public so anyone can find it, and it can be featured in the Squads directory . Companies can use a Verified Company Squad , which adds a verified badge and can post automatically from your RSS feeds and YouTube channels. Suggest a source If you own a well-known developer publication or blogging platform, you can suggest it as a source. daily.dev then picks up its new articles automatically. Company and personal blogs aren't eligible; run a Squad instead. You need 250 reputation to suggest a source. See How to suggest a new source . Get credit for articles daily.dev already picked up If daily.dev already picks up your articles from a publication, link them to your profile to get a Creator badge, notifications and analytics. See Getting credit for articles you wrote . Want more reach? Boost a post with Cores to show it to more developers, labeled as promoted.
