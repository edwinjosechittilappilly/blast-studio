Recommended posts in feed Last updated: 4 weeks ago LinkedIn will recommend posts in your feed that we believe you might find valuable and relevant. These posts may be from people you don&rsquo;t follow or aren&rsquo;t connected to, but that we offer as professionally relevant. You may see social context such as Followed by [name] when one of your connections follows or is connected to the author. You may also see an option to Follow or Connect with the author. How we select recommended content We rely on a variety of signals to select content and decide to whom to display such content. Below are some of the influential signals we rely on when selecting this content: High quality professionally relevant content: Our goal is to suggest to members content that is professionally relevant and high quality, including content discussing: Professional growth, development, and education; Being inspired at work; Professional products or services; and Education on professional topics (e.g., economics or technology). Relevant to all members: When recommending content outside the author&rsquo;s network, we try to only serve content that is valuable and relevant to those outside of the original poster&rsquo;s network by, for example, ensuring it provides sufficient context, isn&rsquo;t overly promotional, and has related elements such as text, images, and video. Feedback: We consider member feedback, including through mechanisms like member disinterest signals and reports about content. We may also review some content to confirm that it is high-quality and professionally relevant. How we deliver Suggested content to you We recommend professionally relevant news and information from a pool of content selected using signals such as those described above. From that pool of content, we also do our best to ensure the recommended content served to you is relevant to you, using information about you and your activity, including: Your previous interactions with content, such as comments, reactions, and time spent on the platform Your profile information, such as job title, profile location, and industry; and How likely you are to engage with the content Your controls over content If you see a post in your feed that you don&rsquo;t think is relevant or useful, you can hide it and provide feedback to help improve your feed experience. Select the More icon on the post and choose the applicable option. Doing so will prevent that specific post from appearing in your feed again.
