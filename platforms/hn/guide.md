# Hacker News

## Who reads it

People evaluating original, substantive links and submissions.

## How distribution works

The [FAQ](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsfaq.html) describes a basic points-and-age ranking with penalties and moderation; it is not a complete current formula. [Guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) require original non-editorialized titles where possible, mark video/PDF, forbid generated post text and AI-edited comments, and ask users not to automate posting or solicit votes or comments. Self-submission must not make promotion the main use. [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html) is for something personally built that people can try now, not a landing page, blog or newsletter.

## Submission etiquette

- The AI writes nothing for HN: no title, post text, comment, draft or outline.
- A person supplies the original title and URL; preserve the original non-editorialized title where possible.
- Check that the source is substantive and original, and identify video or PDF links using the required marker.
- Show HN is for something personally built that people can try now, not a landing page, blog or newsletter.
- Do not automate posting, solicit votes or comments, or make self-promotion the main use.

## Hooks

Do not generate hooks or alternative titles. Review only the human-provided original title.

## Structure template

No generated submission template. The human supplies the original source title and URL.

## Anti-patterns

- Generated submission text or AI-edited comments.
- Editorialized titles, vote solicitation or automated submission.
- A Show HN link to a landing page rather than something people can try.

## AI and disclosure

No generated post text and no AI-edited comments. The AI writes nothing for HN, including no title, draft or outline.

## Checklist

- [ ] Human supplied the original title and URL.
- [ ] Source suitability and any Show HN requirements checked.
- [ ] Video/PDF marker checked when applicable.
- [ ] No generated post text, edited comment or solicitation.
